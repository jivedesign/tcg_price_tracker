How they compare

Correlation uses period-to-period changes over the selected range, against the first selected card (or the basket). Near +1 means they move together, near 0 means unrelated, and negative means they tend to move opposite. Correlation is not causation.

Import your own data (CSV)

Long format with a header row: date,name,price (dates as YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY-MM). Card files: one row per card per date. Stock files: use the ticker as the name.

Where to get real data: Scryfall's bulk data has current prices, so a daily snapshot builds your history. Card price history is also available from sources like MTGGoldfish and TCGplayer, subject to their terms. Stock history can be exported as CSV from most brokers and finance sites.
